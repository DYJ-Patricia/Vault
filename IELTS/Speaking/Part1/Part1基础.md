## 注意同义替换

Who do you live with?

I live with my ==flatmates==, because I am staying in a **dormitory** with another four ==people==.

You know, in China, it is very common for college students to live in **university accommodation** with three to six ==peers==, as there are millions and millions of us. It’s hard for ==everyone== to rent an individual **apartment**.

So, the campus normally comes with **dorms**, which is, I know, quite unusual in Western countries. But I enjoy it; we are all good friends.

## 描述展开

Who do you live with ?

I live with my flatmates because I am staying in a dormitory with another four people.

We are all ==classmates==, and we are quite ==close==. We normally do our ==assignments== together, and during the weekends, sometimes we ==go shopping== together … You know, we quite ==enjoy== living with each other so far.
