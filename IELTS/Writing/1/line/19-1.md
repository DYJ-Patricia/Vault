Overall, the number of participants in the film club and martial arts remained relatively stable throughout the entire period, while the popularity of table tennis and musical performances rose. In contrast, amateur dramatics was the only activity to experience a decline in attendance. Notably, the film club consistently had the highest participation, while musical performances initially had zero attendance during the first five years.

Initially, in 2000, about 65 people participated in the film club, roughly 30 more than those involved in martial arts. Over time, martial arts attendance fluctuated between approximately 30 and 40 participants before returning to its initial value of about 35 participants in 2020. Meanwhile, film club attendance, which started and ended at around 65 participants, experienced a dip to slightly above 60 attendees in 2010.

In 2000, amateur dramatics was slightly more popular than table tennis, with around 28 and 18 attendees, respectively, while musical performances had no participants. Over the next five years, musical performances saw no change in popularity, while amateur dramatics and table tennis slightly increased to nearly 30 and 20 participants, respectively. Subsequently, there were consistent increases in musical performances, reaching slightly less than 20 participants in 2020. Table tennis attendance plateaued at 20 participants until 2010 before skyrocketing to around 55 participants in 2020.
